This is a separate request for Haven, the AI companion app you designed in direction 3a, "Sage & lamplight". The app is built, but it has no landing page: new visitors land straight on the app's Home. Please design a landing page for first-time visitors, in the same visual language.

The attached HANDOFF.md is the brief. Read it fully first. Its ground rules are firm, its content list holds the only facts to use, and "What I need back" lists the formats I need. The screenshots show the current first-visit Home, consent sheet and privacy page.

In short:
- One page for someone who may be upset, at night, on a phone, and doesn't yet know what Haven is or whether to trust it.

- It earns trust. It doesn't sell.
- No testimonials, numbers or promises. Leave a marked placeholder for pricing.
- Get help stays visible.
- It must work at 390 px, in both themes, with WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, 44 px touch targets, and a reduced-motion version.

Please deliver HTML/CSS mockups I can open, driven by the token names. Ask me if anything in the brief is unclear before you go deep.
