Every state has its own motion. The orb tilts in 3D: a soft highlight slides the opposite way to the turn and the eyes move with it, so it reads as a ball rather than a flat disc. When you're talking (listening) and during Breathe it faces you and doesn't turn. With reduced motion turned on, everything holds still.
